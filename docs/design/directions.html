What each one costs

Salon feutré continues the tokens already in place (felt, card stock, brass). The work is the deck grid in card proportions and per-deck sleeve colors, which the roadmap already plans.

Grimoire replaces the palette and adds a third typeface. It reads beautifully as a list, but it is the furthest from a table.

Arène is the most spectacular and the hardest to keep readable: glows and translucent panels must be checked for contrast in both themes, and a light version is hard to make credible.
